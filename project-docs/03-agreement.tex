\section{Project}
Developer will design and build a custom-coded marketing website for Client, per the ``Option 2'' scope agreed by email, summarized below.

\section{Deliverables}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Up to 12 custom-coded pages, mobile responsive (phone, tablet, desktop). Core pages included at launch are: Home, About KUL Enterprises, Services, Safety \& Compliance, Carrier Packet / Documents, Driver Careers, Request a Quote, and Contact.
  \item A no-code content editing system so Client can update text and images without editing code.
  \item Three forms (Request a Freight Quote, General Contact, Driver Employment Inquiry), each delivering submissions to Client's email.
  \item SSL, automated backups, and local SEO (page titles, meta descriptions, sitemap, fast loading, mobile optimization, and local-business schema markup).
  \item Google Analytics and Google Search Console setup.
  \item Google Business Profile connection and map embedding after Client creates and verifies the profile.
  \item A custom opening animation sequence featuring the Jamaican Doctor Bird transforming into the KUL Enterprises logo and tagline, as detailed in the Website Blueprint.
  \item Domain connection assistance (Client purchases the domain in Client's name) and hosting setup on Vercel's free tier in Client's name.
  \item A 60-minute training session and a handoff of the GitHub repository containing all source code.
\end{itemize}

\noindent\textbf{Page Allotment:} The build includes up to 12 pages. Pages not used at launch remain available for Client to add later at no additional cost until the 12-page total is reached. Standard pages beyond 12 are quoted separately, as is any of the future functionality listed in Section 3.

\section{Out of Scope (Future Phases)}
The following are not included and, if pursued, will be scoped and quoted under separate agreements: customer portal, broker portal, shipment tracking, online payments, gated driver or customer resource systems, blog or news system, and any fleet-expansion functionality beyond standard pages. Developer will build on a framework capable of supporting these later without rebuilding the site; this does not include building the features themselves.

\section{Client Responsibilities}
Client will provide, in a timely manner: the project content and final Website Blueprint, logo and brand artwork, company photos, DOT and MC numbers, and any other required text or media; purchase the domain name; create and verify the Google Business Profile; and respond to drafts and questions within a reasonable time (target: 3 business days). The project timeline begins when Client delivers the final Website Blueprint and required assets, not at signing.

\section{Timeline}
Estimated 2 to 3 weeks from receipt of content, with an initial draft within 3 days of starting. Delays in Client deliverables or feedback extend the timeline accordingly.

\section{Revisions}
Four (4) revision rounds are included, conducted over Zoom across the whole site. Additional revision rounds are \$50 each. A ``revision round'' is one consolidated set of change requests reviewed together.

\section{Payment}
Total fee: \$750 USD.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \$375 (50\%) deposit due before work begins (non-refundable; reserves the engagement).
  \item \$375 (50\%) balance due at completion, before final repository transfer and domain go-live.
  \item Payment via Zelle (770-714-0190). Balances unpaid 14 days past due may pause work and will accrue a late fee of 1.5\% per month (or the maximum permitted by law, whichever is less) until paid in full.
\end{itemize}

\section{Intellectual Property}
Upon receipt of final payment, Developer assigns to Client all rights to the work created specifically for this project: website design, source code, custom programming, custom graphics and images, written content produced for the site, any database built for the project, and project files. \textbf{Excluded:} third-party assets, stock media, fonts, and open-source components, which retain their own licenses and are delivered to Client as usage rights; and any pre-existing tools, libraries, or code Developer owned before this project, which Developer may continue to reuse. Developer may display the work in a portfolio and reference Client as a client after the website has publicly launched, unless Client requests otherwise in writing.

\section{Hosting, Domain, and Accounts}
Domain and hosting accounts are created in Client's name and remain Client's property and responsibility. The site is built to run on Vercel's free tier; any future paid upgrades, third-party service fees, or domain renewals are Client's responsibility. Developer charges no mandatory monthly or maintenance fee.

\section{Post-Launch Fixes and Maintenance}
For 30 days after launch, Developer will fix defects in the delivered work at no charge. New features, content changes, and enhancements after handoff are billable at an agreed hourly or per-project rate; because Client owns the code, any developer may also perform them.

\section{Confidentiality}
Each party will keep the other's non-public business information confidential and use it only for this project.

\section{Limitation of Liability}
Developer's total liability under this agreement will not exceed the total fees paid. Developer is not liable for indirect or consequential damages, third-party service outages, or losses arising from Client-provided content.

\section{Mutual Indemnification}
Each party agrees to indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the other party from any claims, losses, or damages arising from materials, assets, or content they provide for the project, including intellectual property infringement.

\section{Independent Contractor}
For the purposes of this Agreement, Developer is an independent contractor, not an employee, partner, or agent of Client.

\section{Force Majeure}
Neither party shall be liable for delays or failures to perform due to causes entirely beyond their reasonable control, such as natural disasters, severe internet outages, or unexpected severe illness.

\section{Termination}
Either party may terminate with written notice. On termination, Client pays for all work completed to date; the deposit is non-refundable. Work completed and paid for is transferred to Client.

\section{Governing Law and Dispute Resolution}
This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Georgia. Prior to initiating litigation, the parties agree to attempt to resolve any dispute through good-faith mediation. Any subsequent legal action shall be resolved exclusively in the state or federal courts located in Douglas County, Georgia.

\section{Entire Agreement}
This document is the full agreement and supersedes prior discussions. Changes must be in writing and signed by both parties.
