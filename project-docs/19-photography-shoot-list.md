# 19. Photography still owed

**For Mark. Written 28 July 2026.**

Every photograph on the site is now one of yours. That was the good news from the
de-stock pass: nothing on it is bought stock any more, and the legal notices page
says so in as many words.

The bad news is what the photographs are *of*. All twenty-six are roads and
landscapes taken through a windscreen. **Not one of them contains freight, a
trailer, a dock, a fifth wheel, a load, or a person.** So the seven service pages
each illustrate themselves with a picture of a road, and a broker reading the
reefer page sees a mountain.

None of this needs a photographer. It needs a phone, an afternoon, and the truck.

---

## Priority 1. The yard afternoon

One session, the truck, about an hour. This closes most of the gap on the site.

**How to take them:** phone is fine, landscape orientation, wipe the lens first.
Stand far enough back that the whole subject fits with room around it, because
the site crops to several shapes. Daylight, no flash. Take three or four of
each and send them all; picking is my job, not yours.

## Priority 2. One portrait

There is **no photograph of you as an adult anywhere in the inbox.** The only
person in the twenty-six is you as a boy in Jamaica.

That is why the About page's founder note sits next to a picture of a road. The
note is signed by you and a photograph of a stranger next to it would read as a
photograph of you, which is not something this site is willing to do.

One portrait, outdoors, next to or in front of the truck, looking at the camera.
Not a studio shot. Somebody else holds the phone.

---

## Priority 3. The Southeast

**No photograph in the archive was taken in Georgia.** The Contact page's
"where we are" card shows an open road that is somewhere in the western high
desert, and its caption used to claim it was a Southeast highway until that was
corrected.

Next time you run I-20 or I-85, one photograph of a shield or a green sign with
an Atlanta distance on it. Thirty seconds of work, and it makes the home region
claim true in a picture instead of only in text.

The dashcam archive on the desktop may already contain this. If a frame can be
pulled from it, no new shot is needed.

---

## Priority 4. The rig from outside

The hero film is computer generated. It is honest about that in the legal
notices, and it stays for now, because it is the only place on the entire site
where the livery appears at size. The dashcam cannot help: it films forward from
inside the cab and can never see the outside of its own truck.

One rolling exterior shot at dusk, taken from another vehicle, would replace it
with the real thing. This is the lowest priority because what is there now is
not misleading, only synthetic.

## Two questions, not photographs

1. **Does KUL actually haul reefer, or broker it?** The reefer page describes a
   recorded set point from pickup to delivery. If that freight is brokered to
   another carrier, the copy needs changing more than the picture does.
2. **Is the FMCSA insurance filing currently active?** The carrier packet page
   links a broker straight to the lookup. If the filing has lapsed, that link
   shows it to every broker who clicks.
