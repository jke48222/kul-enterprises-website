## 1. Project
Developer will design and build a custom-coded marketing website for KUL Enterprises LLC, per the "Option 2" scope agreed by email, summarized below.

## 2. Deliverables
- Up to **12 custom-coded pages**, mobile responsive (phone, tablet, desktop).
- A **no-code content editing system** so Client can update text and images without editing code.
- **Three forms** (Request a Freight Quote, General Contact, Driver Employment Inquiry), each delivering submissions to Client's email.
- **SSL**, automated **backups**, and **basic SEO** (page titles, meta descriptions, sitemap, fast loading, mobile optimization).
- **Google Analytics** and **Google Search Console** setup.
- **Google Business Profile** connection *after* Client creates and verifies the profile.
- Domain connection assistance (Client purchases the domain in Client's name) and **hosting setup on Vercel's free tier in Client's name**.
- A **60-minute training session** and a handoff of the **GitHub repository** containing all source code.

**Page allotment.** The build includes up to 12 pages. Pages not used at launch remain available for Client to add later at no additional cost until the 12-page total is reached. Standard pages beyond 12 are quoted separately, as is any of the future functionality listed in Section 3.

## 3. Out of Scope (Future Phases)
The following are **not** included and, if pursued, will be scoped and quoted under separate agreements: customer portal, broker portal, shipment tracking, online payments, gated driver/customer resource systems, blog/news system, and any fleet-expansion functionality beyond standard pages. Developer will build on a framework capable of supporting these later **without** rebuilding the site; this does not include building the features themselves.

## 4. Client Responsibilities
Client will provide, in a timely manner: the project content/Blueprint, logo and brand artwork, company photos, DOT and MC numbers, and any other required text or media; purchase the domain name; create and verify the Google Business Profile; and respond to drafts and questions within a reasonable time (target: 3 business days). **The project timeline begins when Client delivers the Blueprint and required assets**, not at signing.

## 5. Timeline
