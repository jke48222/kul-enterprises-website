# KUL Enterprises Website — Post-Launch Care

*One page, for Mark. What's covered after launch, what's billable, and how
to reach me. This accompanies the agreement; where they differ, the signed
agreement controls.*

## The 30-day defect window (included, $0)

For 30 days after the site goes live on kulenterprises.com, I fix **defects
in the delivered work** at no charge. A defect is something that worked at
handoff breaking, or something delivered that doesn't work as agreed:

- A form that stops delivering to dispatch@kulenterprises.com
- A page that errors, won't load, or displays wrong on phone/tablet/desktop
- Broken links, images, or the opening animation misbehaving
- Anything in the agreement's deliverables list not working as described

**How to report:** email or text me what you saw, where (page + device),
and a screenshot if you can. I acknowledge the same business day and fix
genuine defects within 2 business days, usually faster.

## What's billable after handoff

New work is not a defect. After handoff these are quoted or billed at an
agreed rate before I start — no surprise invoices:

- New pages beyond the 12-page allotment, new sections, or redesigns
- New features (per the agreement: customer/broker portals, shipment
  tracking, online payments, blog/news, gated resources)
- Photography swaps and content restyling beyond what the /admin editor does
- Third-party integrations (load boards, TMS, accounting)

**Content edits are yours, free, forever:** text, photos, services, and FAQ
are all editable through /admin (see the CMS Editing Guide). You never pay
me to change a phone number.

## Things you own and renew (not me)

- **Domain** (NameCheap, your account) — renews yearly
- **Hosting** (Vercel, your account) — free tier, no renewal
- **Google accounts** (Analytics, Search Console, Business Profile) — yours
- **Resend** (form email delivery, your account) — free tier

I hold no keys you can't revoke; everything runs in accounts you control.

## After the 30 days

There's no mandatory maintenance fee. If you want ongoing help, two simple
options: pay-as-you-go (agreed hourly, quoted before work starts) or a
small monthly retainer we can define when you know your rhythm. And because
you own the code and the GitHub repository outright, any developer you ever
hire can maintain it — you are never locked to me.
