**FIRST MEETING AGENDA**

# The KUL Enterprises Website

Sixty minutes. Mark Brown and Jalen Edusei.

One hour, two goals: your feedback on every page, and kulenterprises.com pointed at the
new site with quote delivery switched on. If you want a head start, the working site is
live at kul-enterprises.vercel.app.

**01. The full site, page by page** (about 35 minutes)
Every page of the live site, top to bottom, at reading pace. Your reactions drive the
work that follows: anything you want changed goes on a written punch list before we move
on. Nothing is final until you have seen it and said so.

**02. Your domain, connected live** (about 15 minutes)
We point kulenterprises.com at the new site together, then switch on email delivery so
quote and contact submissions reach your inbox. We finish by sending a real test quote
and watching it arrive. Your email keeps working exactly as it does today.

**03. What the site still needs from you** (about 8 minutes)
Three short sittings on your own schedule: an hour at a computer for the remaining
accounts, an hour with the truck and a phone camera using the shot list I will hand you,
and a few business details to put on record.

**04. Next steps** (final 2 minutes)
We schedule your editing training session, then set the launch target together.

**Please have ready**

- Your Squarespace login, the account where kulenterprises.com lives.
- Access to the dispatch@kulenterprises.com inbox during the meeting.
- Your phone, to see the site the way a broker first will.
