# Style 01 — Cinematic Noir

Aesthetic: cinematic noir — warm charcoal, champagne-gold accents, low-light gradient scenes,
cinematic vehicle photography, frosted-glass cards, ghost wordmark, modern grotesque with
generous tracking

Reference: assets-inbox/inspiration/cinematic-noir-dribbble.jpeg — match feel, not content.

Intent: A night-drive showroom. The visitor walks into a private viewing of a serious
machine — hushed, warm, expensive, unhurried. Trust arrives as composure: nothing shouts,
everything is lit deliberately. KUL as the Mercedes of one-truck carriers.

Guardrails: Always gold-family accents on deep charcoal (#0B0B0B–#2E2E2E) with warm gradient
light, real photography over illustration, one statement line per viewport, generous negative
space. Never neon, never flat dead-black (light always has temperature), never more than one
gold accent element per view, never bird imagery.

## Build notes

- Mixed-ground rhythm like the ref: dark cinematic hero and contact, lighter editorial middle.
- Statement section with floating photo cluster: "Freight is never just freight." energy.
- Services as a glass-card carousel over a blurred fleet/road backdrop.
- Footer: giant ghost "KUL" wordmark fading into the dark.

## Tweaks to expose

gold saturation/dosage · dark:light section ratio · glass blur radius · hero photo treatment
(dusk vs night) · statement-line typeface · gradient warmth
