# Style 05 — Fog Monument

Aesthetic: fog monument — fog-white ground, monumental quiet grotesque, one glowing gold
accent per viewport, faint geometric line-grid, tiny monospace annotations, engineering
poetry

Reference: assets-inbox/inspiration/soft-premium-dribbble.jpeg + soft-premium-2 / -3 / -4
(all one brand system) — match feel, not content.

Intent: The most disciplined room in the multiverse. Vast calm space, one enormous
soft-spoken statement, one gold glow. Precision as emotion — the ref's "Every millimeter
earns its place" becomes KUL's "Every mile taught a lesson." The visitor trusts KUL the way
they trust an instrument: because nothing here is careless.

Guardrails: Always ≥60% empty space, exactly one glow accent per viewport, monospace
annotations for hard facts (USDOT/MC as precision specs), soft-focus photography with gold
halation. Never dark sections (this is the light pole of the multiverse), never more than two
type scales per viewport, never decorative icons.
