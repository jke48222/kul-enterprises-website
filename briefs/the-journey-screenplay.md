# The Journey — Mark's Screenplay (received Jul 23, 2026)

Source: Mark's email "The Journey Section" — the screenplay + creative direction for the
documentary-style chapter of the KUL site. His framing: "a creative blueprint that communicates
the emotion, pacing, and experience," not rigid instructions; Jalen's creativity welcomed as long
as "the heart of the story remains intact." Every scene must answer: **"Why does this matter?"**
(not "what happened"). On-screen copy below is verbatim — copy is sacred; prose descriptions are
condensed.

## Timing vocabulary (his spec)

- **Pause** 1–2s — absorb a sentence. **Hold** 3–5s — reflection. **Long Pause** 5–8s — the
  heaviest moments, used sparingly.
- **Fade** 1.5–2.5s dissolve. **Fade to Black** 2–4s — chapter reset. **Slow Reveal** 2–3s —
  nothing appears suddenly. **Crossfade** 2–3s between photographs.
- **Silence is intentional.** Music (Akilah's) supports, never leads.
- Pacing test: "If viewers instinctively want to click ahead, the pacing is too slow. If they
  don't have time to absorb the message, the pacing is too fast."

## His three rules

1. Every scene answers "Why does this matter?"
2. The visitor should never feel like they're reading — short sentences, breathing room,
   photography, gentle transitions, silence, music only when it adds.
3. **Never exaggerate.** The story carries its own weight; tell it honestly.

## Scenes (on-screen copy verbatim)

**S1 — Every Road Has a Beginning.** Black. Silence, 3s. → "Every company has a story." /
"Ours began long before the first truck." / "It began with character." → Doctor Bird glides,
gold trail, lion emerges (the intro sequence) → logo + tagline → "Every road has a beginning." /
"This is mine."

**S2 — Jamaica.** Dark ground, Jamaica/childhood photo slot. "I was born in Jamaica." / "On
August 8, 1988..." / "My mother brought my sister and me to America." / "She told us we were
spending the summer with our father." / (long pause) "We never went back." — stands alone.
Lesson: "Sometimes life's greatest journeys begin without our permission."

**S3 — Learning the Value of Work.** Warm morning light; construction/family photo or texture.
"America was different." / "Everything moved faster." / "Everything felt unfamiliar." / "While
most kids spent their summers playing..." / "Mine were spent on construction sites." / "Every
school break..." / "Every holiday..." / "Every vacation..." / "I worked alongside my father." /
"At the time..." / "I didn't understand why." / "I only knew that it was expected." →
"Looking back..." / "Those days taught me something I couldn't see at the time." / "Work isn't
something to avoid." / "It's something to take pride in." Lesson: "Character is often built long
before opportunity arrives." (Emotion: quiet respect. Type: large, centered, minimal.)

**S4 — Seeing the World Differently.** Brightening; quiet road to horizon. "As I grew older..." /
"I began to notice something." / "I wasn't satisfied with simply being told how things were." /
"I wanted to understand why they worked the way they did." / "Questions became part of the
journey." → "Curiosity doesn't always give you immediate answers." / "Sometimes..." / "It gives
you a lifetime of learning." Lesson: "Every road begins with a question."

**S5 — The Beginning of Independence.** Quiet road at sunrise. "Every life reaches a moment..." /
"When someone else stops making the decisions." / "This was mine." / "Leaving home wasn't just
about finding a place to live." / "It was about finding out who I was." / "For the first
time..." / "I was making my own decisions." / "Some were good." / "Some weren't." / "But every
one of them belonged to me." → "Independence isn't the freedom to avoid mistakes." / "It's
accepting responsibility for them." Lesson: "Growth begins the moment excuses end."

**S6 — Choosing a Greater Challenge.** Wider horizon; fuller (not louder) music. "Independence
gave me freedom." / "But freedom also brought a question." / "What kind of man did I want to
become?" / "I wanted structure." / "I internally searching for purpose." / "I wanted to be
challenged." / "That's what led me to the United States Air Force." → "Discipline isn't about
being controlled." / "It's about learning to control yourself." Lesson: "Character grows when
comfort is replaced with commitment." (Air Force photos take precedence when provided; avoid
glorifying equipment — service and commitment, not hardware.)

**S7 — Forged Through Discipline.** Steady deliberate pacing. "Some lessons can only be
learned..." / "through discipline." / "The Air Force challenged me in ways I had never
experienced before." / "It demanded consistency." / "Accountability." / "Commitment." / "Every
day became another opportunity..." / "to become a little stronger than the day before." →
"Discipline doesn't change who you are." / "It reveals who you're willing to become." Lesson:
"Excellence is rarely built in a single moment." / "It's built one decision at a time." (Ends in
quiet confidence, no celebration.)

**S8 — Life Doesn't Follow a Straight Line.** Desert highway, road bends, destination hidden.
"I thought I knew where life was taking me." / "Like many people..." / "I had a plan." / "But
plans have a way..." / "of changing." / "Some opportunities opened new doors." / "Others closed
before I expected." / "There were victories." / "There were setbacks." / "Both became teachers."
→ "Life rarely asks whether you're ready." / "It simply asks..." / "What will you do now?"
Lesson: "Resilience isn't refusing to fall." / "It's choosing to keep moving." (This is where
visitors begin to trust — honesty over perfection.)

**S9 — Earning Trust.** Busier highway, more lanes; people appear. "Opportunity has a way..." /
"of finding people who are prepared for it." / "I wasn't chasing titles." / "I was learning to
become dependable." / "One responsibility became another." / "One opportunity led to the next." /
"Before long..." / "I found myself leading others." → "Leadership isn't about being in front." /
"It's about being someone others can count on." Lesson: "Trust is earned long before it's ever
expected." (Camera rises slightly — leadership as broader perspective, moving WITH others.)

**S10 — The Road Became My Teacher.** His strongest endless-highway photo; passenger-seat feel.
"I thought I was learning how to drive a truck". / "What I didn't realize..." / "was that the
road was teaching me something far greater." / "Every mile introduced me to a new place." /
"Every state introduced me to different people." / "Every delivery reminded me..." / "That trust
travels farther than freight." / "I saw mountains..." / "deserts..." / "cities that never seemed
to sleep..." / "and quiet roads where time felt like it had stopped." / "The country was
changing." / "And so was I." → photo flow: sunrise → mountain pass → snow highway → desert →
coastline → storm → open interstate (slow crossfades, each image breathes). → "The farther I
traveled..." / "the more I realized..." / "Every person has a story." Lesson: "The road doesn't
just take you to new places." / "It teaches you to see the world differently."

**S11 — A Vision Begins to Form.** Sunrise beginning; driver knows where he's going. "The longer
I traveled..." / "The more I began to notice something." / "Freight moved every day." / "But what
truly kept the industry moving..." / "wasn't trucks." / "It was people." / "Drivers." /
"Dispatchers." / "Mechanics." / "Customers." / "Brokers." / "Families waiting at home." / "Every
load represented someone's trust." / "Every mile represented someone's promise." / "That's when a
vision began to take shape." / (long pause) "What if a company could be built..." / "Where
integrity wasn't just a word..." / "but the standard?" → first subtle lion element (watermark,
not logo reveal — an idea, not a company). → "A business begins with an idea." / "A lasting
business begins with values." Lesson: "Companies are remembered for what they deliver." / "Great
companies are remembered for how they make people feel."

**S12 — More Than a Business.** Pre-dawn stillness; first time NO movement. "There came a
moment..." / "when I stopped asking..." / "What kind of business do I want to own?" / (long
pause) "I began asking a different question." / "What kind of company deserves to exist?" / "A
company built on honesty..." / "when honesty costs something." / "A company built on
integrity..." / "even when no one is watching." / "A company where safety isn't measured by
regulations alone..." / "but by the value placed on every person who trusts us." / "I
realized..." / "I wasn't trying to build a trucking company." / (long pause) "I was trying to
build something people could believe in." → first sunlight crosses the highway. → "Businesses
are created every day." / "Trust..." / "is built one decision at a time." Lesson: "The strongest
foundation isn't concrete." / "It's character." (Emotion: a promise made to oneself.)

**S13 — The Promise.** Full daylight, clear horizon. "Every company begins with paperwork." /
"But that's not where KUL began." / "KUL began with a promise." / "A promise..." / "to do
business with integrity." / "To treat people with respect." / "To never compromise safety for
convenience." / "To remember..." / "that behind every shipment..." / "every invoice..." / "every
phone call..." / "is a person who placed their trust in us." / "That promise became the
foundation." / "The trucks would come later." → close-up: hands writing goals/values in a
notebook (imagining the company before building it). → "A company's reputation isn't created by
advertising." / "It's created by the decisions no one else sees." Lesson: "Principles are
promises you keep..." / "even when they're difficult."

**S14 — Meet KUL.** Sunrise complete; logo appears simply, confidently. "Every mile..." / "Every
lesson..." / "Every decision..." / "Led here." / "Welcome to KUL Enterprises." / (long pause)
"We didn't build this company to be the biggest." / "We built it to be trusted." / "We
believe..." / "Integrity is earned." / "Safety is personal." / "Communication matters." / "Every
customer deserves honesty." / "Every driver deserves respect." / "Every load deserves our full
attention." → logo moves aside; the website reveals itself one section at a time, like opening a
book. → "Values don't appear on a balance sheet." / "But they're often the most valuable asset a
company owns." Lesson: "Reputation isn't built by what a company says." / "It's built by what
people experience." (His best professional truck photo belongs here when it exists.)

**S15 — What We Stand For.** Clean bright confident; ordinary moments done exceptionally well
(pre-dawn inspection, professional phone call, update, handshake, safe arrival). "Every company
has values." / "Ours were learned..." / "long before this company existed." → values as promises,
one at a time: **Integrity** "We do the right thing... even when no one is watching." /
**Safety** "Every load matters. Every person matters more." / **Communication** "Trust grows when
people know what's happening." / **Respect** "We believe relationships are built... one
conversation at a time." / **Excellence** "Good enough has never been our destination." /
**Accountability** "When we make a promise... We intend to keep it." → "Values don't matter
because they're written." / "They matter because they're lived." Lesson: "Character isn't
something a company advertises." / "It's something people experience."

**S16 — The Road Ahead.** Aerial winding highway, morning light. "Every road leads somewhere." /
"Some journeys last a few miles." / "Others..." / "shape an entire lifetime." / "KUL Enterprises
is still writing its story." / "One shipment at a time." / "One relationship at a time." / "One
promise kept at a time." / "We don't know every opportunity that lies ahead." / "But we do know
the principles that will guide us there." / "Integrity." / "Character." / "Safety." /
"Service." / "Trust." → tagline: "Strength in Motion." / "Built on Integrity." / "Driven by
Safety." → final line beneath: **"Built by Character. Trusted Through Action."** → "A destination
gives us direction." / "Character determines how we get there." Lesson: "The journey never really
ends." / "It simply becomes someone else's beginning." (Akilah's music reaches its emotional
peak here — warmth, not volume.)
**Optional coda he floated:** after fade to black + 2s silence, the gold Doctor Bird appears one
last time — perched, looks toward the horizon, takes flight; as it leaves, the KUL lion remains.
No words. *(NOTE: conflicts with the locked bird-appears-once law from Jul 11 — needs Mark's

**S17 — The Next Journey.** Nearly still; sun fully up; no destination visible. "Every mile I've
traveled..." / "Every lesson I've learned..." / "Every promise I've chosen to keep..." / "Has led
to this moment." / "Not because my journey is complete." / "But because it's finally ready..." /
"to serve yours." / (silence) "Trust isn't given." / "It's earned." / "One mile." / "One
decision." / "One relationship at a time." → lion fades in alone → "KUL Enterprises" /
"Strength in Motion." / "Built on Integrity." / "Driven by Safety." → several still seconds →
"Thank you..." / "for taking this journey with us." / "Whenever you're ready..." / "we'll be
