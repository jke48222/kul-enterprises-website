# Style 04 — Heritage Institute

Aesthetic: heritage institute — warm cream ground, deep forest and earth greens with gold,
hand-drawn line-art landscape bands woven between photo grids, humane serif-accented
typography, mission-driven storybook warmth

Reference: assets-inbox/inspiration/editorial-documentary-2-dribbble.jpeg — match feel, not
content.

Intent: KUL as an institution in the making — a company with roots, values, and a long view.
The Jamaican heritage and the tree/roots/legacy symbolism live here through warmth and
illustration, never through cliché. It feels like a place with history, not a pitch.

Guardrails: Always cream ground with earth-tone accents + gold (earth tones are blessed in
Mark's original palette), illustration as connective tissue between real photos — never
replacing them, humane generous line-height. Never tropical kitsch, never flag/palm clichés,
never cartoon mascots — the lion appears only as a refined mark.

## Build notes

- Illustrated rolling-road/horizon band as the recurring section divider (Mark's horizon
  motif, hand-drawn).
- Values as engraved plaques; six core values from the brand board.
- Vision/"Road Ahead" as a growth-rings or planted-tree moment tied to the big-tree photo.
- Journey teaser pairs the river photo with one story line.

## Tweaks to expose

illustration density · green:gold balance · serif vs grotesque headline mix · paper-grain
texture amount · plaque styling · divider frequency
