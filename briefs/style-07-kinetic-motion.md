# Style 07 — Kinetic Motion

Aesthetic: kinetic motion — full-bleed graded road footage under massive white overlay type,
black letterbox frame, frosted-glass HUD stat cards, scroll-driven word reveals, warm golden
grade

Reference: assets-inbox/inspiration/kinetic-motion-dribbble.mp4 (primary) — match feel, not
content. (Stills in scratchpad; watch for the type-assembly timing.)

Intent: The site IS the windshield. Real dashcam footage runs under type that arrives with
momentum — "STRENGTH / IN / MOTION" assembling as you scroll. Glass HUD cards carry live
numbers (miles, USDOT, 24/7). The visitor feels forward motion for the entire visit.
Trust = a company that is already moving.

Guardrails: Always footage graded and dimmed so type stays effortlessly readable, black
letterbox frame, white type with gold HUD accents, scroll-LINKED with native scroll preserved,
reduced-motion fallback = graded still + pre-assembled type. Never scroll-jacking, never
autoplay audio, never UI over busy unreadable footage. Until Mark's dashcam clips land there is no
real footage available — use a slow Ken-Burns graded pan over a journey photo as the motion
placeholder (no fake stock footage), clearly marked PLACEHOLDER in code; the real dashcam
loop slots straight in when the clips arrive.

## Build notes

- This family is the natural home of the dashcam-loop concept from Mark's brief.
- HUD card: glass panel with a route line (Loganville → nationwide), USDOT/MC, 24/7 dispatch.
- Services as HUD chips that surface as the road rolls.
- Mobile: footage swaps to a lighter loop or graded still — never heavy video on cellular.
