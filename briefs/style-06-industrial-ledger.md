# Style 06 — Industrial Ledger

Aesthetic: industrial ledger — Swiss-industrial spec sheet, bento/mondrian panel grid with
visible seams, bracketed [↘] annotations, cool gray ground with saturated gold blocks,
grotesque headlines, engineering-catalog captions, date stamps

Reference: assets-inbox/inspiration/instrustrial-ledger-dribbble.mp4 — match feel, not
content. (Stills in scratchpad; the ref's international orange becomes KUL gold.)

Intent: KUL as precision equipment. The page reads like the spec sheet of a company that
measures everything — services as catalog modules, safety as tolerances, the operation as an
engineered product. Brokers feel process. Trust = rigor made visible.

Guardrails: Always a hard grid with visible panel seams, at most one full-gold panel per
screen, mono/bracketed annotations, real data only. Never soft friendly shadows or rounded
corners, never metaphorical imagery — freight, road, machine, and material only.

## Build notes

- Services as the ref's module row (three spec cards with icons + dense captions), extended
  to all seven services via a horizontal rail.
- Trust bar as a stamped spec strip: USDOT · MC · INSURED · EST. GA.
- Vision as a dated ledger row: "2029-12 — 50 tractors. Status: building."
- One gold "Request a Quote" panel placed like the ref's News & Story block.

## Tweaks to expose

seam visibility · gold-block frequency · bracket/corner-mark style · panel density · gray
temperature · stamp treatment
