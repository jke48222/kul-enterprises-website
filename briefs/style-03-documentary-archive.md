# Style 03 — Documentary Archive

Aesthetic: documentary archive — photo-journal plates, monospace/typewriter captions,
date-stamped entries, full-bleed landscapes alternating with white gallery pages, oversized
grotesque section titles

Reference: assets-inbox/inspiration/editorial-documentary-dribbble.mp4 — match feel, not
content. (Stills: scratchpad frames; watch the clip for the plate→gallery pacing.)

Intent: Mark's miles as an archive being opened for the visitor. Every photograph reads as
evidence — dated, placed, kept. The page feels like leafing through a driver's records:
quiet, factual, deeply human. Trust = receipts.

Guardrails: Always Mark's real photographs as full-bleed plates with mono uppercase captions
(date · place · lesson), white gallery pages between plates, gold used sparingly like an
archival stamp. Never stock imagery, never decorative flourishes, never invented dates or
places — use honest TBD markers until facts arrive from Mark.

## Build notes

- Hero: full-bleed journey photo, mono overlay intro, small archival stamp mark.
- Trust data rendered as a manifest/ledger entry (mono grid).
- Services as typewritten index cards.
- Vision as the final dated entry: "2029 — the fleet this road is building."

## Tweaks to expose

caption density · plate:gallery rhythm · stamp-gold dosage · photo grade (neutral vs warm
archival) · title scale · mono face choice
