# Style 02 — Marquee Editorial

Aesthetic: marquee editorial — edge-to-edge condensed uppercase marquee type, duotone
photography, indexed labels ((01), (002)), hairline rules, full-bleed image bands,
cool-premium restraint

Reference: assets-inbox/inspiration/cinematic-noir-2-dribbble.jpeg — match feel, not content.

Intent: A fashion-magazine spread for freight. Type IS the visual — the tagline runs the
viewport edge like a marquee, services read as an indexed editorial catalog. Confidence
through typography and cropping, not effects. Curated, current, sharp.

Guardrails: Always charcoal/white ground with gold as the temperature accent (replacing the
ref's blue), duotone or graded photography only, every image indexed or captioned, max two
display sizes per viewport beyond body text. Never rounded-bubbly type, never
centered-everything symmetry, never raw unprocessed snapshots.

## Build notes

- Hero: "STRENGTH IN MOTION" (or "KUL") cropped off-canvas as a marquee over a duotone
  truck/landscape plate.
- Services as numbered editorial rows 01–07 with hairline separators.
- Trust strip as a spec line in small caps between hairlines.
- Journey teaser as a full-bleed duotone plate with an indexed caption.

## Tweaks to expose

marquee crop amount · duotone tint (gold vs steel) · index/label style · image grade dosage ·
hairline weight · uppercase tracking
