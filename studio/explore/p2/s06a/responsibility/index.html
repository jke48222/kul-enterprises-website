§02 — Written standards

Four disciplines,
held daily.

Compliance is the floor, not the ceiling. These are the standards we keep because the freight — and the people around the truck — deserve them.

D-01 — Maintenance

Fix it before it fails.

Maintenance here is scheduled, not deferred. The truck is inspected before and after every run, and anything found gets corrected before the next load — because a deadline is never worth a defect on the road. A machine that is cared for is the first safety device on the vehicle.

Pre-trip + post-trip · defects corrected first
D-02 — Hours

The clock is a safety device.

Hours-of-service rules exist because tired drivers hurt people. We treat the limits as a design constraint, not an obstacle: routes are planned around legal rest from the start, so no load ever depends on a driver pushing past the clock. If the ETA and the rules conflict, the ETA moves.

Planned around rest · never against it
D-03 — Communication

Reachable, around the clock.

A safety culture you can't reach isn't one you can trust. Dispatch answers 24/7 — pickup, transit, delivery, or two in the morning when something changes. Status updates go out before you think to ask, and if there's a problem, you hear it from us first, with the plan attached.

24/7 — 678-972-1148 · a person answers
D-04 — Cargo

Your freight, our name.

From the moment the doors close, the load is our responsibility — sealed and documented at pickup, secured to standard, photographed at both ends of the run, and handed back with the paperwork matching the freight. We carry it the way we'd want our own carried.

Sealed · secured · documented · delivered

Anyone can write the word "safety" on a website. Public numbers, logged hours, and filed inspection reports are harder to fake — so that's what we put forward. If anything here doesn't check out, call and tell us. We'll want to know more than you do.
