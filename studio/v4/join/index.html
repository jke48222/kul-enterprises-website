06.2 / The commitments

Promises we can make
before the first hire

Pay, routes and particulars belong to the opening that carries them. These four do not — they are true of the truck that is running today, so they can be written down today.

01 / Maintained equipment

You will never be asked to run a truck the founder would not run himself. Pre-trip findings get fixed, not filed, and a truck that should not roll does not roll — even when the load is worth more than the repair.

02 / Legal hours, always

Your logbook is yours. Nobody here will lean on you to stretch a clock. When the window and the hours disagree, the load re-plans and the customer gets told — the driver is not the part that bends.

03 / Dispatch that answers

The 24/7 number our shippers call is the same one you will call. A person picks it up — nights, weekends and the awkward hours — and that person can make a decision without escalating it.

04 / Straight announcements

Pay structure, routes and particulars get published with each opening: accurate, complete, and honored exactly as written. If a number is not settled yet, this page says so instead of inventing one.

[ 05 ] — And one we will not make

We will not post a pay figure before there is a truck and a lane behind it. No invented numbers before their time — that is the whole point of this page.

06.3 / The process

Four conversations,
not a funnel

When a seat opens, this is the whole of it. No panel interviews, no personality test, and no silence at the end — you get an answer either way.

01

You reach out

Email or call. One sentence about what you drive now and where you want to be home is enough to start.

02

We talk, properly

Twenty minutes on the phone with the person who would dispatch you. Ask about pay, home time and the equipment — you get straight answers or none.

03

You see the truck

Come to Loganville. Walk around it, read the maintenance record, and take it out if the paperwork allows. Judge the equipment yourself.

04

Terms in writing

Pay structure, home time and lanes on paper before you resign anything. What is written is what is honored.
