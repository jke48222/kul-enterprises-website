The honest version: we're not hiring yet.

Today, KUL Enterprises is one truck, run by its founder. We would rather tell you that plainly than post a job that doesn't exist. What we're doing now is laying the ground that future drivers will stand on: the lanes, the standards, and the reputation.

Growth is planned, not hoped for. As tractors are added, driving opportunities will be announced here and to everyone on the notify list. Every seat added will be a seat worth having.

1→50

One truck today, fifty tractors by the end of 2029. That's the plan in writing, and it's the same one we show our customers.

What driving for KUL will mean.

No invented pay tables, no promises we can't sign yet. Just the standards this company already runs on, which every future driver inherits.

Hours respected, not squeezed

The founder drives by legal, honest hours of service. Nobody who joins will ever be pushed to run tired: the schedule is built around rest, not against it.

Equipment that's cared for

Maintenance is done before it's due, not after something fails. You'll drive a truck that gets the same attention we ask you to give the freight.

Straight talk, both ways

Dispatch answers, tells the truth, and backs its drivers. The same 24/7 communication our customers get is the culture inside the company too.

Growth you can see

Joining early in a fleet that's building toward fifty tractors means growing with it. The plan is public, and so is the progress.
