Chapter eight · Where this is going

The road
ahead.

One truck today. Fifty tractors by the end of 2029. Here is how we intend to get there, honestly.

The fleet plan: one truck today, fifty tractors by the end of 2029

1Truck on the road today 50Tractors by end of 2029

The number is in writing so you can hold us to it. Everything between one and fifty will be earned the same way the first truck was: one load, one promise, one customer at a time.

Growing with intention.

Plenty of carriers grow fast and lose the thing that made them worth calling. We would rather grow slower and keep it. So we keep two lists.

What grows

  • The fleetFrom one tractor toward fifty, added only as fast as we can staff and maintain them properly.
  • The lanesSoutheast roots first, then the corridors our customers actually need.
  • The driversProfessionals who treat freight the way our founder did across roughly eleven years behind the wheel.
  • The relationshipsShippers and brokers who started with one load and stayed.

What never changes

  • The pre-tripEvery truck, every departure, whether we run one tractor or fifty.
  • The answered phoneDispatch that picks up, at any size. You will never be routed into silence.
  • The honest ETAWe would rather tell you a hard truth early than a soft one late.
  • The standardSafety measured by the value placed on every person who trusts us, not by regulations alone.

How a one-truck carrier earns a fleet.

Not by pretending to be big. By being worth growing. These are the commitments the next forty-nine trucks will be built on.

  • One customer at a time

    We win freight by running the first load well, not by promising capacity we don't have. Give us one load and judge us on it.

  • Add trucks slower than we could

    A tractor joins the fleet only when there's a driver we trust to run it and the work to keep it moving. Growth never outruns the standard.

  • Say no to freight we can't run right

    If we can't cover a lane safely and on time, we tell you before you book, not after you've committed.

  • Stay on the record

    Every truck we add runs under the same verifiable authority: USDOT 7638788, MC 66389691. Look us up at any size.

Every great fleet begins with one truck, one customer, and one promise kept.

The truck is on the road. The promise is in writing. The customer could be you.
