Jamaica

I was born in Jamaica.

On August 8, 1988...

My mother brought my sister and me to America.

She told us we were spending the summer with our father.

We never went back.

Sometimes life's greatest journeys begin without our permission.

Learning the Value of Work

America was different.

Everything moved faster.

Everything felt unfamiliar.

While most kids spent their summers playing...

Mine were spent on construction sites.

Every school break...

Every holiday...

Every vacation...

I worked alongside my father.

At the time...

I didn't understand why.

I only knew that it was expected.

Looking back...

Those days taught me something I couldn't see at the time.

Work isn't something to avoid.

It's something to take pride in.

Character is often built long before opportunity arrives.

Seeing the World Differently

As I grew older...

I began to notice something.

I wasn't satisfied with simply being told how things were.

I wanted to understand why they worked the way they did.

Questions became part of the journey.

Curiosity doesn't always give you immediate answers.

Sometimes...

It gives you a lifetime of learning.

Every road begins with a question.

The Beginning of Independence

Every life reaches a moment...

When someone else stops making the decisions.

This was mine.

Leaving home wasn't just about finding a place to live.

It was about finding out who I was.

For the first time...

I was making my own decisions.

Some were good.

Some weren't.

But every one of them belonged to me.

Independence isn't the freedom to avoid mistakes.

It's accepting responsibility for them.

Growth begins the moment excuses end.

Choosing a Greater Challenge

Independence gave me freedom.

But freedom also brought a question.

What kind of man did I want to become?

I wanted structure.

I internally searching for purpose.

I wanted to be challenged.

That's what led me to the United States Air Force.

Air Force years · photographs to come

Discipline isn't about being controlled.

It's about learning to control yourself.

Character grows when comfort is replaced with commitment.

Forged Through Discipline

Some lessons can only be learned...

through discipline.

The Air Force challenged me in ways I had never experienced before.

It demanded consistency.

Accountability.

Commitment.

Every day became another opportunity...

to become a little stronger than the day before.

Discipline doesn't change who you are.

It reveals who you're willing to become.

Excellence is rarely built in a single moment.
It's built one decision at a time.

Life Doesn't Follow a Straight Line

I thought I knew where life was taking me.

Like many people...

I had a plan.

But plans have a way...

of changing.

Some opportunities opened new doors.

Others closed before I expected.

There were victories.

There were setbacks.

Both became teachers.

Life rarely asks whether you're ready.

It simply asks...

What will you do now?

Resilience isn't refusing to fall.
It's choosing to keep moving.

Earning Trust

Opportunity has a way...

of finding people who are prepared for it.

I wasn't chasing titles.

I was learning to become dependable.

One responsibility became another.

One opportunity led to the next.

Before long...

I found myself leading others.

Leadership isn't about being in front.

It's about being someone others can count on.

Trust is earned long before it's ever expected.

The Road Became My Teacher

I thought I was learning how to drive a truck.

What I didn't realize...

was that the road was teaching me something far greater.

Every mile introduced me to a new place.

Every state introduced me to different people.

Every delivery reminded me...

That trust travels farther than freight.

cities that never seemed to sleep...

The farther I traveled...

the more I realized...

Every person has a story.

The road doesn't just take you to new places.
It teaches you to see the world differently.

A Vision Begins to Form

The longer I traveled...

The more I began to notice something.

Freight moved every day.

But what truly kept the industry moving...

wasn't trucks.

It was people.

Drivers.

Dispatchers.

Mechanics.

Customers.

Brokers.

Families waiting at home.

Every load represented someone's trust.

Every mile represented someone's promise.

That's when a vision began to take shape.

What if a company could be built...

Where integrity wasn't just a word...

but the standard?

A business begins with an idea.

A lasting business begins with values.

Companies are remembered for what they deliver.
Great companies are remembered for how they make people feel.

More Than a Business

There came a moment...

when I stopped asking...

What kind of business do I want to own?

I began asking a different question.

What kind of company deserves to exist?

A company built on honesty...

when honesty costs something.

A company built on integrity...

even when no one is watching.

A company where safety isn't measured by regulations alone...

but by the value placed on every person who trusts us.

I realized...

I wasn't trying to build a trucking company.

I was trying to build something people could believe in.

Businesses are created every day.

Trust...

is built one decision at a time.

The strongest foundation isn't concrete.
It's character.
