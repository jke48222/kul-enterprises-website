A true story · told in seventeen scenes

The Journey.

Before KUL Enterprises was a trucking company, it was a road — from Jamaica to Georgia, construction summers to the United States Air Force to the open interstate. This is that road. Scroll when you’re ready, and take your time.

Scene 01

Every Road Has a Beginning

Every company has a story.
Ours began long before the first truck.
It began with character.
KUL Enterprises Strength in Motion · Built on Integrity · Driven by Safety
Every road has a beginning.This is mine.

Scene 02

Jamaica

I was born in Jamaica.
On August 8, 1988…
My mother brought my sister and me to America.
She told us we were spending the summer with our father.
We never went back.

Sometimes life’s greatest journeys begin without our permission.

Scene 03

Learning the Value of Work

America was different.Everything moved faster.Everything felt unfamiliar.
While most kids spent their summers playing…Mine were spent on construction sites.
Every school break…Every holiday…Every vacation…
I worked alongside my father.
At the time…I didn’t understand why.I only knew that it was expected.
Looking back…Those days taught me something I couldn’t see at the time.
Work isn’t something to avoid.It’s something to take pride in.

Character is often built long before opportunity arrives.

Scene 04

Seeing the World Differently

As I grew older…I began to notice something.
I wasn’t satisfied with simply being told how things were.
I wanted to understand why they worked the way they did.
Questions became part of the journey.
Curiosity doesn’t always give you immediate answers.Sometimes…It gives you a lifetime of learning.

Every road begins with a question.

Scene 05

The Beginning of Independence

Every life reaches a moment…When someone else stops making the decisions.
This was mine.
Leaving home wasn’t just about finding a place to live.It was about finding out who I was.
For the first time…I was making my own decisions.
Some were good.Some weren’t.But every one of them belonged to me.
Independence isn’t the freedom to avoid mistakes.It’s accepting responsibility for them.

Growth begins the moment excuses end.

Scene 06

Choosing a Greater Challenge

Independence gave me freedom.But freedom also brought a question.
What kind of man did I want to become?
I wanted structure.I internally searching for purpose.I wanted to be challenged.
That’s what led me to the United States Air Force.
Discipline isn’t about being controlled.It’s about learning to control yourself.

Character grows when comfort is replaced with commitment.

Scene 07

Forged Through Discipline

Some lessons can only be learned…through discipline.
The Air Force challenged me in ways I had never experienced before.
It demandedConsistency.Accountability.Commitment.
Every day became another opportunity…to become a little stronger than the day before.
Discipline doesn’t change who you are.It reveals who you’re willing to become.

Excellence is rarely built in a single moment. It’s built one decision at a time.

Scene 08

Life Doesn’t Follow a Straight Line

I thought I knew where life was taking me.
Like many people…I had a plan.
But plans have a way…of changing.
Some opportunities opened new doors.Others closed before I expected.
There were victories.There were setbacks.Both became teachers.
Life rarely asks whether you’re ready.It simply asks…What will you do now?

Resilience isn’t refusing to fall. It’s choosing to keep moving.

Scene 09

Earning Trust

Opportunity has a way…of finding people who are prepared for it.
I wasn’t chasing titles.I was learning to become dependable.
One responsibility became another.One opportunity led to the next.
Before long…I found myself leading others.
Leadership isn’t about being in front.It’s about being someone others can count on.

Trust is earned long before it’s ever expected.

Scene 10

The Road Became My Teacher

I thought I was learning how to drive a truck.
What I didn’t realize…was that the road was teaching me something far greater.
Every mile introduced me to a new place.Every state introduced me to different people.
Every delivery reminded me…That trust travels farther than freight.
I saw mountains…deserts…cities that never seemed to sleep…and quiet roads where time felt like it had stopped.
The country was changing.And so was I.
The farther I traveled…the more I realized…Every person has a story.

Photographs: Mark’s own miles

Five photographs from Mark’s own miles — the places the road showed him.

The road doesn’t just take you to new places. It teaches you to see the world differently.

Scene 11

A Vision Begins to Form

The longer I traveled…The more I began to notice something.
Freight moved every day.But what truly kept the industry moving…wasn’t trucks.It was people.
Drivers.Dispatchers.Mechanics.Customers.Brokers.Families waiting at home.
Every load represented someone’s trust.Every mile represented someone’s promise.
That’s when a vision began to take shape.
What if a company could be built…Where integrity wasn’t just a word…but the standard?
A business begins with an idea.A lasting business begins with values.

Companies are remembered for what they deliver. Great companies are remembered for how they make people feel.

Scene 12

More Than a Business

There came a moment…when I stopped asking…
What kind of business do I want to own?
I began asking a different question.
What kind of company deserves to exist?
A company built on honesty…when honesty costs something.
A company built on integrity…even when no one is watching.
A company where safety isn’t measured by regulations alone…but by the value placed on every person who trusts us.
I realized…I wasn’t trying to build a trucking company.I was trying to build something people could believe in.
Businesses are created every day.Trust…is built one decision at a time.

The strongest foundation isn’t concrete. It’s character.

Scene 13

The Promise

Every company begins with paperwork.But that’s not where KUL began.
KUL began with a promise.
A promise…to do business with integrity.To treat people with respect.To never compromise safety for convenience.
To remember…that behind every shipment…every invoice…every phone call…is a person who placed their trust in us.
That promise became the foundation.The trucks would come later.
A company’s reputation isn’t created by advertising.It’s created by the decisions no one else sees.

Principles are promises you keep… even when they’re difficult.

Scene 14

Meet KUL

Every mile…Every lesson…Every decision…Led here.
Welcome to KUL Enterprises.
We didn’t build this company to be the biggest.We built it to be trusted.
We believe…Integrity is earned.Safety is personal.Communication matters.Every customer deserves honesty.Every driver deserves respect.Every load deserves our full attention.
Values don’t appear on a balance sheet.But they’re often the most valuable asset a company owns.

Reputation isn’t built by what a company says. It’s built by what people experience.
