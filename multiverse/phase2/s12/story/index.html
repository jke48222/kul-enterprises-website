The Founder’s Story

Why I started
driving.

Every company has a story.

Ours began long before the first truck.

It began with character.

Told in seven chapters
Scroll
I Jamaica

I was born in Jamaica.

On August 8, 1988…

My mother brought my sister and me to America.

She told us we were spending the summer with our father.

We never went back.

The lesson

Sometimes life’s greatest journeys begin without our permission.

II Learning the Value of Work

America was different. Everything moved faster.

While most kids spent their summers playing…

Mine were spent on construction sites.

Every school break. Every holiday. Every vacation.

I worked alongside my father.

At the time, I didn’t understand why. I only knew that it was expected. Looking back, those days taught me something I couldn’t see at the time: work isn’t something to avoid. It’s something to take pride in.

The lesson

Character is often built long before opportunity arrives.

“Every road begins with a question.”

III The Beginning of Independence

Every life reaches a moment when someone else stops making the decisions.

This was mine.

For the first time, I was making my own decisions.

Some were good. Some weren’t.

But every one of them belonged to me.

Leaving home wasn’t just about finding a place to live. It was about finding out who I was. Independence isn’t the freedom to avoid mistakes — it’s accepting responsibility for them.

The lesson

Growth begins the moment excuses end.

IV Forged Through Discipline

I wanted structure. I wanted to be challenged.

That’s what led me to the United States Air Force.

It demanded consistency. Accountability. Commitment.

Every day became another opportunity to become a little stronger than the day before.

Discipline isn’t about being controlled. It’s about learning to control yourself. Discipline doesn’t change who you are — it reveals who you’re willing to become.

The lesson

Excellence is rarely built in a single moment. It’s built one decision at a time.

V Life Doesn’t Follow a Straight Line

I thought I knew where life was taking me. Like many people, I had a plan.

But plans have a way of changing.

There were victories. There were setbacks.

Both became teachers.

Life rarely asks whether you’re ready. It simply asks: what will you do now?

The lesson

Resilience isn’t refusing to fall. It’s choosing to keep moving.

VI Earning Trust

I wasn’t chasing titles.

I was learning to become dependable.

One responsibility became another. One opportunity led to the next.

Before long, I found myself leading others.

Leadership isn’t about being in front. It’s about being someone others can count on.

The lesson

Trust is earned long before it’s ever expected.

Chapter VII · The Road Became My Teacher

Eleven years behind the wheel — and every mile had something to say.

Close to eleven years of commercial driving. Every mile introduced me to a new place. Every state introduced me to different people. Every delivery reminded me that trust travels farther than freight. The country was changing — and so was I.
