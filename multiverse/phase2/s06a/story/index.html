The Journey — opening lines

Entry E-01

1988-08-08

Arrival.

Jamaica → United States

Origin — on file

I was born in Jamaica.

On August 8, 1988...

My mother brought my sister and me to America.

She told us we were spending the summer with our father.

— long pause —

We never went back.

Lesson — entered Sometimes life's greatest journeys begin without our permission.

Entry E-02

School breaks · holidays · vacations

Learning the value of work.

Construction sites, alongside his father

Trade — construction

America was different. Everything moved faster. Everything felt unfamiliar.

While most kids spent their summers playing...

Mine were spent on construction sites.

Every school break... every holiday... every vacation... I worked alongside my father.

At the time, I didn't understand why. I only knew that it was expected.

Work isn't something to avoid.

It's something to take pride in.

Lesson — entered Character is often built long before opportunity arrives.
Plate reserved — P-02 Construction summers · imagery pending from the founder.

Between entries

As I grew older, I wasn't satisfied with simply being told how things were. I wanted to understand why they worked the way they did.

Every road begins with a question

Entry E-03

The first door of his own

The beginning of independence.

Leaving home

Decisions — his own

Leaving home wasn't just about finding a place to live.

It was about finding out who I was.

For the first time, I was making my own decisions. Some were good. Some weren't.

But every one of them belonged to me.

Independence isn't the freedom to avoid mistakes.

It's accepting responsibility for them.

Lesson — entered Growth begins the moment excuses end.

Entry E-04

United States Air Force

Choosing a greater challenge.

Service — structure, purpose, discipline

USAF — served

Independence gave me freedom. But freedom also brought a question.

What kind of man did I want to become?

I wanted structure. I wanted to be challenged.

That's what led me to the United States Air Force.

It demanded consistency. Accountability. Commitment.

Every day became another opportunity to become a little stronger than the day before.

Discipline doesn't change who you are.

It reveals who you're willing to become.

Lesson — entered Excellence is rarely built in a single moment. It's built one decision at a time.
Plate reserved — P-03 Air Force years · service photographs take precedence when provided.

Note in the margin

No hardware on this page — the service mattered for the commitment, not the equipment.

Entry E-05

The plan, revised

Life doesn't follow a straight line.

Doors opened · doors closed

Setbacks — kept on file

Like many people, I had a plan. But plans have a way of changing.

Some opportunities opened new doors. Others closed before I expected.

There were victories. There were setbacks.

Both became teachers.

Life rarely asks whether you're ready. It simply asks — what will you do now?

Resilience isn't refusing to fall.

It's choosing to keep moving.

This entry stays in the record on purpose. A company that only files its victories is hiding something; honesty over perfection is where trust begins.

Field note

The wave arrives whether you are ready or not. He photographed it because he had lived it.

Both became teachers

Entry E-06

One responsibility, then another

Earning trust.

Leadership — arrived unannounced

Dependable — on record

I wasn't chasing titles.

I was learning to become dependable.

One responsibility became another. One opportunity led to the next.

Before long, I found myself leading others.

Leadership isn't about being in front.

It's about being someone others can count on.

Lesson — entered Trust is earned long before it's ever expected.

Entry E-07

≈ 11 years · commercial driving

The road became my teacher.

Lower 48 — seen from the driver's seat

CDL — the long way

I thought I was learning how to drive a truck.

What I didn't realize was that the road was teaching me something far greater.

Every mile introduced me to a new place. Every state introduced me to different people.

Every delivery reminded me that trust travels farther than freight.

The country was changing. And so was I.

The road doesn't just take you to new places.

It teaches you to see the world differently.

Roughly eleven years of commercial driving sit behind this record — the miles that turned a driver into a founder. The full photographic account lives in The Journey.
