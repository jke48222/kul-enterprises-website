§02 — Read before joining

What a seat here will mean.

Pay and benefits get published when hiring opens — real numbers, not teasers. What we can promise now is the part that doesn't change with the market: how drivers get treated.

Written before the first hire
T-01

Equipment you don't have to fight.

Maintenance is scheduled, not deferred. You will never be asked to run a truck with a known defect — the same standard the company already holds with one truck and its own name in the logbook.

T-02

Legal hours, respected — always.

Routes are planned around the federal clock from the start. No dispatcher here will ever lean on you to push past it, because the schedule was never built on breaking it.

T-03

Dispatch that answers you, too.

The 24/7 line brokers get is the same line drivers get. Questions answered, problems owned, plans communicated — in both directions.

T-04

Your name on the standard.

Fifty trucks is the plan; the standard is the product. Early drivers won't inherit a culture — they'll help set it, with the founder still in a seat beside them.
