The Standard

Four responsibilities. Zero shortcuts.

Safety here isn’t a poster in the break room. It’s four disciplines we accept on every dispatch — written down so you can hold us to them.

R—01 · Maintenance

The truck is ready before the load is.

Maintenance is a schedule, not a reaction. Our equipment is inspected before it rolls and checked again when it parks, and a defect gets fixed before it gets driven.

  • Pre-trip and post-trip inspections on every dispatch
  • Scheduled preventive maintenance — not run-to-failure
  • A defect grounds the truck until it’s resolved
R—02 · Hours of Service

We respect the clock — both of them.

Hours-of-service rules exist because tired drivers hurt people. We run legal hours, period — which means the ETA we give you is one a rested driver can actually keep.

  • Hours-of-service compliance on every run, no pushed windows
  • ETAs quoted from legal drive time, not wishful thinking
  • If a window can’t be made safely, you hear it before booking
R—03 · Communication

You always know where your freight is.

Silence is how small carriers lose trust. Our dispatch line is answered around the clock, and a delay is reported the moment we know about it — not after you call asking.

  • 24/7 dispatch — 678-972-1148 answers nights and weekends
  • Status at pickup, in transit, and at delivery
  • Problems reported first by us, not discovered by you
R—04 · Cargo

Your freight rides like it’s ours.

Cargo responsibility starts at the dock: secured properly, sealed when it should be, documented at both ends. What leaves the shipper is what arrives at the receiver.

  • Load securement checked before the doors close
  • Seals respected and recorded end to end
  • Clean paperwork — BOL signed, exceptions noted honestly

The safest carrier is the one you never have to think twice about.
