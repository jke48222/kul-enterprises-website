The KUL Lineup

Freight, handled every way you haul it.

No riddles, no upsell. Seven services, each explained the way a broker reads a load board: what it is, what it’s for, and who should book it.

01

Power Only

Drop & hook · Tractor + driver

You already have the trailer — loaded, staged, ready. We supply the tractor and a professional driver, hook to your equipment, and move it. Clean drop-and-hook, no dwell games.

Book it when

You run trailer pools or pre-loaded trailers and need dependable tractor capacity without committing your own power.

Request this lane →
02

Dry Van

53′ van · General freight

Standard 53-foot dry-van capacity for palletized, boxed, and general commodity freight. Sealed at the dock, secured for the road, delivered the way it was loaded.

Book it when

Your freight is non-perishable and dock-to-dock — consumer goods, packaging, building products, no-touch loads.

Request this lane →
03

Reefer

Temperature controlled

Refrigerated capacity for temperature-sensitive freight. The set point is confirmed before the doors close, held through the run, and checked at every stop — continuous cool from dock to dock.

Book it when

The load has a temperature on the rate confirmation: food-grade, produce, or anything that spoils if the reefer isn’t honest.

Request this lane →
04

Dedicated

Contracted · Repeatable

Committed capacity on your lane — same carrier, same standard, on a schedule you can plan a dock around. Your freight stops being a daily search and becomes a settled line item.

Book it when

You move the same lane week after week and want one accountable carrier instead of a new name every Monday.

Request this lane →
05

Regional

Southeast · Short haul

Quick-turn runs out of Loganville, Georgia and across the Southeast corridor. Short lanes, tight windows, local knowledge — this is home ground, and it shows in the transit times.

Book it when

Your lane lives in the Southeast — Georgia and its neighbors — and you want a carrier based inside it, not passing through.

Request this lane →
06

Over-the-Road

Long haul · Nationwide

Coast-to-coast capacity for the long lanes. Interstate authority nationwide, honest ETAs, and a dispatch line that answers at 2 a.m. the same as it does at 2 p.m.

Book it when

The load leaves the region — cross-country moves where communication across days matters as much as the truck.

Request this lane →
07

Expedited

Time-critical · Direct

When the clock is the whole job, we run it direct: pick up, drive, deliver. No relay hand-offs, no waiting on a consolidation — one truck committed to one deadline.

Book it when

A line is down, a window is closing, or the cost of late is bigger than the cost of the truck.

Request this lane →
