Chapter 06 · Join the Journey

The seats are coming.
The standard is here.

KUL is building a fleet worth driving for — slowly, honestly, one tractor at a time. If you want to be behind the wheel when the doors open, this is where the journey starts.

Get notified first → See the plan

Straight Talk First

We’re not hiring yet — and we’ll say so plainly.

Today, KUL Enterprises runs one truck, and the founder is the one driving it. We won’t post ghost jobs, collect résumés for a seat that doesn’t exist, or promise pay we haven’t set.

What we will do: grow with intention, and announce every driver opportunity the moment a tractor is added. When we call, it’ll be about a real truck with your name on the door assignment.

The Growth Plan

Seats open as the fleet grows.

No dates we can’t keep, no counts we can’t prove. This is the honest shape of the road between one truck and fifty.

Today

One truck, founder-driven.

Every mile is run by the man whose name is on the authority. This is where the standard gets set — the maintenance discipline, the communication habits, the reputation the fleet will inherit.

As we grow

Tractors added with intention.

Each new tractor is earned by the freight before it — and each one opens a driver’s seat. Opportunities are announced as equipment is added, never before. No stockpiled applications, no waiting lists that go nowhere.

End of 2029

Fifty tractors. Fifty professionals.

The goal on the wall: a fifty-tractor fleet where every driver works the way the first truck did — rested, respected, and backed by dispatch that answers. That culture is being built now, before the second truck ever arrives.

Driving for KUL

What a seat here will mean.

We can’t quote pay or benefits we haven’t set — we won’t invent them. What we can promise is the culture the first truck already runs on.

J—01

Your clock is respected.

Legal hours, honest schedules, and no dispatcher pushing you past what’s safe. The founder drives — he knows what the seat feels like.

J—02

Your equipment is maintained.

Pre-trip discipline and preventive maintenance mean you’re never asked to run a truck we wouldn’t run ourselves.

J—03

Your dispatch answers.

The same 24/7 line our shippers trust is the one that picks up for you — at 2 a.m., in the rain, no exceptions.

J—04

Your name means something.

You’d be joining a carrier building its reputation mile by mile — and every driver who joins becomes part of that story, not a truck number.

Be First to Know

When the seats open, hear it first.

Leave your email and we’ll reach out when driver opportunities are announced — and only then. No spam, no newsletters, just the call you’re waiting for.

This is a preview build — the list isn’t wired up yet. Want to introduce yourself today? Email dispatch@kulenterprises.com and it goes straight to the founder.
