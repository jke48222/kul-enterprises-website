The honest part

We are not hiring yet — and we will not pretend otherwise

Plenty of carrier sites promise the moon to drivers before they have the trucks to back it up. That is not how KUL Enterprises works. Today we run one truck, and the seat in it is taken — by the founder.

What we are doing is building: earning lanes, keeping our safety record clean, and growing revenue with the discipline it takes to add tractors the right way. As each truck joins the fleet on the road toward fifty by the end of 2029, a driver's seat opens with it — and those opportunities will be announced here first.

No open positions today. No fabricated pay packages. Just a growing carrier that will tell you the truth now so you can trust it later — when there is a seat with your name on it.

— KUL Enterprises, Loganville, Georgia
