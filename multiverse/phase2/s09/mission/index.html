The service ledger

Direct answers,
before you ever call

Our primary operations run across the Southeast out of Loganville, Georgia, with the authority and appetite to cover lanes nationwide. Here is each service, what it means in practice, and where it fits.

01Trailer supplied by you

Power Only

You have the loaded trailer — we bring the tractor and the driver. We hook to your van, reefer, or flat, move it where it needs to go, and drop it clean. Ideal when your trailer pool is positioned but your power is stretched thin.

Good fit for

  • Drop-and-hook networks and trailer pools
  • Repositioning empty or preloaded trailers
  • Surge coverage when fleet power runs short
Quote a power-only move →
02Enclosed freight

Dry Van

Standard enclosed trailer freight — palletized, boxed, or floor-loaded goods that need to travel sealed, dry, and secure. The workhorse of the industry, run with door-to-door communication from pickup through proof of delivery.

Good fit for

  • Palletized consumer and industrial goods
  • Non-perishable food, paper, and packaging
  • Retail and distribution-center freight
Quote a dry van load →
03Temperature-controlled

Reefer

Refrigerated freight held to your temperature spec from the moment it is loaded until the receiver signs. We confirm the setpoint at pickup, watch it in transit, and communicate — because with temp-sensitive freight, silence is a risk.

Good fit for

  • Produce, chilled, and frozen food loads
  • Temperature-protected goods in extreme weather
  • Freight where spec and paper trail both matter
Quote a reefer load →
04Committed capacity

Dedicated

Capacity committed to your recurring lanes — same standards, same communication, a rhythm you can plan around. Instead of hunting the spot market every week, you have a carrier who already knows your dock, your hours, and your freight.

Good fit for

  • Weekly or repeating lane commitments
  • Shippers who value consistency over churn
  • Building a long-term carrier relationship
Talk about a dedicated lane →
05Southeast primary

Regional

Our home turf. Short and mid-length hauls across Georgia and the surrounding Southeast, where knowing the lanes, the docks, and the traffic means quicker turns and tighter windows. This is where KUL runs most and knows best.

Good fit for

  • Georgia and Southeast lanes with fast turnarounds
  • Next-day and short-haul delivery windows
  • Shippers who want a carrier close to home
Quote a regional lane →
06Long-haul nationwide

Over-the-Road

Long-haul freight moved coast to coast under our own federal authority. Longer lanes mean more that can go quiet — so we do not go quiet. You get honest transit estimates up front and steady check-ins from origin to receiver.

Good fit for

  • Cross-country and multi-day lanes
  • Freight leaving or returning to the Southeast
  • Shippers who expect updates without asking
Quote an OTR lane →
07Time-critical

Expedited

For the load that cannot wait: dispatched fast, driven direct, and delivered inside the legal limits that keep it safe. We will tell you honestly whether your window is achievable — and if we commit to it, we run it like it is ours.

Good fit for

  • Line-down and production-critical freight
  • Missed-connection recovery loads
  • Hard appointment times that cannot slip
Quote an expedited move →
