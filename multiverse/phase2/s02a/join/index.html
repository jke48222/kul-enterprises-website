(Chapter 06) — Join the Journey Driver careers · Written honestly

The fleet is coming. So are the seats.

(Straight talk)We are not hiring yet — and we won't pretend otherwise.

KUL runs one tractor today, and the founder is in it. The plan is fifty tractors by the end of 2029, and every one of them will need a professional in the seat. Opportunities will be announced as tractors are added — no ghost listings, no bait postings. This page exists so that when the seats open, the right people already know us.

(Now) One Truck

The founder drives, dispatches, and answers the phone. Every mile run today is building the reputation — and the revenue — that funds the next tractor. Status — building the record

(Next) Tractor by Tractor

Each tractor added is a seat announced. When a truck joins the fleet, the opening is posted here and sent to everyone on the notify list — real openings only, with real details attached. Status — seats announced as tractors are added

(2029) Fifty Strong

The written goal: fifty tractors by the end of 2029, staffed by drivers who are treated the way the founder wished every carrier treated its drivers. Status — the destination, in writing

(A Note) — To the Drivers We Haven't Met From the founder's desk

A company built by a driver, for drivers.

I know what it feels like to be a number on someone's board — to have your hours pushed, your truck neglected, and your questions ignored. KUL exists partly because of that feeling.

So here's the commitment, made before the first hire: when you drive for KUL, you'll drive maintained equipment, run legal hours, and talk to a dispatcher who answers. The rest of the details will come with the openings — and they'll be real when they do.

— KUL Enterprises LLC · Loganville, Georgia
