What happens after you reach out.

Three steps, the same every time, whether you typed it or called it in.

STEP 01

It reaches dispatch.

Your details go to the person who assigns the truck, not to a shared inbox nobody owns. Phone and email both land in the same place.

Read by a personNot queued, not routed.
STEP 02

A person prices it or answers it.

Equipment, transit plan, and a rate for your specific lane. If the load isn't one we can run properly, you get told that instead of a quote we can't honor.

Priced honestlyIncluding the word no.
STEP 03

It goes in writing before the wheels turn.

Whatever gets agreed on the phone is confirmed in writing: rate, equipment, pickup, delivery. Then you hear from us at pickup, in transit, and at delivery.

ConfirmedNothing lives only in a phone call.
One honest caveat

We don't publish a response-time promise, because the truthful answer depends on what is on the road that day and we would rather not print a number we might miss. What we can say: the dispatch line is staffed 24 hours. If you need an answer inside the hour, call it in rather than type it.
