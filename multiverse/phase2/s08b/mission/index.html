The service ledger, in plain broker language.

No lane is a mystery box. Here is what each service means, how it runs, and when it's the right call for your freight.

The equipment

3 services
01

Power Only

You have the loaded trailer; we bring the tractor and the driver. We hook to your equipment, haul it where it needs to go, and drop it clean. No re-handling of your freight.

Good fit when: you run your own trailers or drop lots and need reliable power to keep them moving.

02

Dry Van

Standard enclosed-trailer freight: palletized goods, packaged product, general commodities. Loaded, secured, sealed, and delivered dry and intact, on the schedule we agreed to.

Good fit when: your freight needs weather protection and a clean, no-drama pickup-to-delivery run.

03

Reefer

Temperature-controlled hauling for loads that spoil, melt, or fail out of range. The set point is confirmed at pickup, held through transit, and documented at delivery.

Good fit when: food or any temp-sensitive product where "close enough" becomes a claim.

The range

2 services
04

Regional

Short-haul work across the Southeast, our home ground. Tighter windows, faster turns, and a driver who knows the docks, the traffic, and the shippers on these lanes because he runs them every week.

Good fit when: your freight lives in the Southeast and you want a carrier that treats it as home territory, not a detour.

05

Over-the-Road

Long-haul, coast-to-coast capacity under full nationwide authority. Trip-planned before the wheels turn, tracked in transit, and communicated at every milestone until the freight is signed for.

Good fit when: the load has to cross the country and you want one accountable carrier the whole way.

The commitment

2 services
06

Dedicated

A truck committed to your lanes, week after week. Same equipment, same standard, a schedule built around your dock. Your freight stops competing with the spot market for capacity.

Good fit when: you have recurring volume and are tired of re-sourcing the same lane every Monday.

07

Expedited

Time-critical shipments moved as the priority, not the backfill. Direct runs, honest transit estimates before we commit, and proactive updates until the receiver signs.

Good fit when: the clock is the whole job: line-down parts, contract deadlines, must-arrive dates.
