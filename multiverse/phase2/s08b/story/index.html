Why I started driving.

The founder's story, told the way it happened. No highlight reel. Seven moments that decided what kind of company this would be.

Every word is true. That was the one rule for this page.

Jamaica · August 8, 1988

A journey that began without permission.

He was born in Jamaica. In the summer of 1988, his mother brought him and his sister to America.

"My mother brought my sister and me to America." "She told us we were spending the summer with our father." "We never went back."
Lesson

Sometimes life's greatest journeys begin without our permission.

Photograph reserved · Jamaica, 1988
School breaks · Construction sites

Summers spent learning what work is.

America moved faster than anything he had known. And while other kids spent their breaks playing, his were spent on construction sites, working alongside his father. Every school break. Every holiday. Every vacation.

"At the time, I didn't understand why. I only knew that it was expected." "Work isn't something to avoid. It's something to take pride in."
Lesson

Character is often built long before opportunity arrives.

Photograph reserved · The construction years
Leaving home

The first decisions that were his.

Leaving home wasn't just about finding a place to live. It was about finding out who he was, and owning every decision that followed.

"Some were good. Some weren't. But every one of them belonged to me." "Independence isn't the freedom to avoid mistakes. It's accepting responsibility for them."
Lesson

Growth begins the moment excuses end.

United States Air Force

Choosing a greater challenge.

Freedom brought a question: what kind of man did he want to become? He wanted structure. He wanted purpose. He wanted to be challenged. That's what led him to the United States Air Force, where every day became another opportunity to be a little stronger than the day before.

ConsistencyAccountabilityCommitment
"Discipline isn't about being controlled. It's about learning to control yourself."
Lesson

Excellence is rarely built in a single moment. It's built one decision at a time.

Photograph reserved · Air Force service
The plan changes

Life doesn't follow a straight line.

Like most people, he had a plan. Some opportunities opened new doors. Others closed before he expected them to.

"There were victories. There were setbacks. Both became teachers." "Life rarely asks whether you're ready. It simply asks: what will you do now?"
Lesson

Resilience isn't refusing to fall. It's choosing to keep moving.

Earning trust

Leadership arrived quietly.

He wasn't chasing titles. He was learning to become dependable. One responsibility became another, one opportunity led to the next, and before long he found himself leading others.

"Leadership isn't about being in front. It's about being someone others can count on."
Lesson

Trust is earned long before it's ever expected.

About eleven years behind the wheel

The road became the teacher.

Around eleven years of commercial driving followed. Every mile introduced a new place. Every state introduced different people. Every delivery taught the same thing, over and over.

"I thought I was learning how to drive a truck. What I didn't realize was that the road was teaching me something far greater." "Trust travels farther than freight."
Lesson

The road doesn't just take you to new places. It teaches you to see the world differently.

Every mile taught a lesson.
Every lesson helped build KUL Enterprises.
