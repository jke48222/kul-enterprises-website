Where we stand, exactly.

Most carriers round up. We would rather show you the real number and let the record grow on its own. Each mark below is one tractor in the 2029 fleet.

Today. One truck, on the road. End of 2029. Fifty tractors, earned one at a time.

No investor countdown, no inflated fleet page. When a mark turns gold on this page, it will be because a real tractor is working real freight.

Growing with intention.

Fifty tractors is not the goal by itself. Fifty tractors that keep the standard of one is. These four commitments scale before the fleet does.

Safety first, always

The record grows before the fleet does.

Every tractor we add inherits the same rule the first one runs on: pre-trip and post-trip inspections logged before the wheels turn, hours of service kept honest.

Retained customers

We expand on kept promises, not borrowed hype.

New trucks get added when existing customers bring us back, load after load. That is slower. It is also the only growth that holds.

Drivers worth trusting

Every seat goes to someone we would put our name behind.

The founder spent about eleven years behind the wheel. Nobody joins this fleet who wouldn't be trusted with the load the way he was.

Dispatch that answers

At fifty trucks, the phone still gets picked up.

Growth that makes a carrier harder to reach is not growth. Dispatch stays reachable 24/7, and you still get a straight answer from a real person.

One promise kept at a time

Every great fleet begins with one truck, one customer, and one promise kept.
