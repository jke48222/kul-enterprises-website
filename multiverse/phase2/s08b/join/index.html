The seats aren't built yet. The standard is.

KUL runs one truck today and isn't hiring drivers right now. But the fleet is growing on purpose, and when it does, we want to already know your name.

We'd rather tell you the truth than post a fake job listing.

Plenty of carriers advertise seats they don't have to collect applications they'll never read. KUL is one truck, driven by the owner, growing toward fifty tractors by the end of 2029. As tractors are added, driving opportunities will be announced, and the people on our notify list hear first.

The straight version: no openings today, real openings coming as the fleet grows. When we post a seat, it will be a seat that exists.

What driving for KUL will mean, whenever you start.

Equipment you can trust
The maintenance discipline we run today scales with the fleet. You'll drive equipment that's inspected every trip and fixed before it fails, because the owner drove under the same rule.
A clock that's respected
Hours of service treated as protection, not an obstacle. Trip plans built around legal, rested windows. Nobody here will ever ask you to outrun your logbook.
Dispatch that answers
The same 24/7 communication our customers get is what our drivers get. A problem at 3 a.m. reaches a person, not a voicemail.

Pay, routes, and benefits will be published with each opening when it's real, not promised in advance on a careers page. That's the same honesty we'll owe you on the road.
